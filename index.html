Equations you can follow

1 · Water-side referenceQref = GrPSG × (Tr_PSG − TrPSG) / 1000

Water flow in t/h; temperature in °C; heat in Gcal/h. Water heat capacity is approximated as 1 kcal/(kg·°C). These three tags are excluded from heat-model inputs.

2 · Heat meter and correctionDt = D0 − Dsp − 3.5
hfg ≈ (2500.9 − 2.36 TcPSG) / 4.1868
Qphysics = Dt × hfg / 1000
Qvirtual = Qphysics + cQ(X)

The inherited neural correction has 23 features and 32/16 hidden units. Direct tag exclusion is verified; proxy connections such as G_HOV still need plant provenance checks.

3 · Separate D0 estimateD0physics = [Dsp(hsp − ht) + DKPU(hKPU − ht) + 860 N/ηem] / (h0 − ht)
D0virtual = D0physics + cD(XwithoutD0)

This follows mass and energy balances in kcal/kg, t/h and MW. All steam enthalpies use h(T) ≈ (2500.9 + 1.82 T)/4.1868; ht includes an assumed 1% moisture adjustment. DKPU = 3.5 t/h, ηem = 0.94. These are engineering proxies, not verified steam properties. The new correction uses 180 depth-2 boosted trees and SE-weighted raw D0 labels; it excludes D0, its derivatives and all three heat-reference tags.

4 · Two warning mechanismsCorrection warning: |cQ| > calibration 99th percentile of |cQ|
Residual warning: |Qref − Qvirtual − calibration center| > τ
τ = max(calibration 99th percentile, 4 × robust σ)

Thresholds are frozen before holdout evaluation. A correction warning means the learned adjustment is unusual; it cannot identify a faulty input. Residual observers provide additional, fallible channel-level evidence. Model correction and SE measurement correction are different quantities.

5 · Fuel-cost contextSteam energy = D0 × (h0 − hfeedwater) / 1000 [GJ/h]
Fuel = steam energy / (boiler efficiency × LHV) [t/h]
Cost = fuel × fuel price [RUB/h]

Here enthalpies use kJ/kg and fuel LHV uses MJ/kg (= GJ/t). Fuel cost covers the steam stream supplying electricity and heat together, not a heat-only bill. Shared boilers, other steam users, blowdown and variable efficiency need plant-specific accounting.
